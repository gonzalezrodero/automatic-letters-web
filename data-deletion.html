How to delete your data:

  1. Open your WhatsApp chat with the bot.
  2. Send a message with the exact phrase below:

(You can also use the command DELETE DATA or ESBORRAR DADES).

What happens next?

My automated systems will immediately intercept this command. It will permanently and securely erase all your conversation records and your phone number from my databases across all instances. The bot will reply with a confirmation message once the process is complete.
